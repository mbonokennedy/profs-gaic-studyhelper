# Planning for Exams

A good exam plan starts with a list of every subject and topic that will be examined. Rank each topic as red (I do not understand it), orange (I partly understand it) or green (I can do it confidently). Spend most of your time on red and orange topics.

Break study time into focused blocks of 25 to 45 minutes with short breaks, known as the Pomodoro technique. Sleep, water and short walks improve concentration more than extra hours of tired studying.

Example: A learner with four weeks before the final exams plans two red topics and one orange topic per day, and reserves every Sunday for a mixed past paper under timed conditions.
